**Category**: SEO | **Severity**: HIGH  
**File**: `src/layouts/Layout.astro`

No `<link rel="canonical">` tag. Every page lacks its canonical URL, risking duplicate content penalties. Astro's `Astro.url` can generate dynamic canonicals.

**Category**: SEO | **Severity**: HIGH | **Status**: ✅ Fixed (2026-06-03)
